🌍 Wazobia - Check Your Breast In Your Language

How To Check - 5 Steps

1. Get Ready: Choose a regular time, like when you shower. If you have periods, a few days after your period ends may be more comfortable.

2. Look in Mirror: Arms relaxed, then raised. Look for change in size, shape, skin dimpling, or change around nipple.

3. Feel: Use pads of fingers, small circles. Cover whole breast, around nipple, up to collarbone, into armpit. Light then firmer.

4. Notice What Is New: New lump, thickening, swelling, nipple turned inward, unexpected discharge, rash, persistent pain.

5. Get Checked Promptly: See a health professional, even if painless. Don't wait for next check.

Go to clinic quickly if you see: New lump, change in size/shape, skin dimpling, nipple turned inward, discharge, rash, or pain that persists.

How To Check Your Breast - 5 Steps (Pidgin)

🩺 Cervical Cancer Risk Check - Wazobia

For education only. Not a diagnosis. Answer 4 questions to see if you should book a Pap Smear.

What is Pap Smear? A test where a health worker checks cells from the cervix (mouth of womb) to find changes early, before cancer develops. It is quick and can save life.

Wetin be Pap Smear? Na test wey health worker go check cells from cervix (mouth of womb) to find wahala early, before e turn cancer. E dey quick and e fit save life.

Menene Pap Smear? Gwaji ne da ma'aikacin lafiya ke duba seloli daga cervix (bakin mahaifa) don gano canji da wuri, kafin ya zama ciwon daji. Gwaji ne mai sauki kuma zai iya ceton rai.

Kini Pap Smear? Idanwo ni ti oṣiṣẹ ilera n ṣayẹwo awọn sẹẹli lati inu cervix (ẹnu ile-ọmọ) lati wa iyipada ni kutukutu, ṣaaju ki o to di aarun jejere. O yara ati o le gba ẹmi là.

Gịnị bụ Pap Smear? Nnwale ebe onye ọrụ ahụike na-enyocha mkpụrụ ndụ site na cervix (ọnụ akpa nwa) ịchọta mgbanwe n'oge, tupu ọ ghọọ ọrịa kansa. Ọ dị ngwa ngwa ma nwee ike ịzọpụta ndụ.
